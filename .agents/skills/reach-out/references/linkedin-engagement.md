Outreach to someone who engaged on LinkedIn — posted themselves, or liked/commented on a followed profile's post. A warm-ish motion: they showed interest in a topic, not in the user's product.

## The angle

The engagement earned a conversation about the topic, nothing more. The first DM talks about what they said or reacted to, as one professional to another; the product earns its mention later, if the conversation goes there.

## Do

- Reach out inside ~48 hours; after that the post is forgotten and the reference reads as scraping.
- Engage with the substance: what the post argued, what their comment added. Have a reaction — agree, push back gently, extend the point.
- Weight authors over reactors: someone who wrote about a problem is a far stronger target than someone who tapped a like. Prioritize the queue accordingly.
- Keep DMs DM-sized: first message well under 500 characters, follow-ups shorter. One idea per message.
- End with a light ask — their take on the topic, or an offer to share something related. A question they can answer in one line.
- If not connected, the connection request goes first — blank by default; the DM after acceptance does the work.

## Don't

- Don't say "saw you liked/commented on" — surveillance framing. Reference the post's substance, not their tracked action.
- Don't pitch in the first DM. Selling in message one converts a warm signal back into cold outreach.
- Don't paste an email into a DM — no subject-line thinking, no paragraphs, no signature.
- Don't chase an ignored connection request over email; being followed across channels is worse than being missed.
- Don't manufacture enthusiasm for a post that says nothing — if there's no substance to engage with, this motion doesn't apply; use another hook.
