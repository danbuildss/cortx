## Instructions

Drives expansion prioritization. Trial accounts route here because the underlying frame is the same as customers — usage depth replaces engagement depth; expansion headroom is the same question.

---

### Step 1 — Assess engagement depth (Q1)

Judge depth by deliberate, post-onboarding behavior only. What counts as depth:

- **Integrations or connections** configured beyond onboarding defaults → deliberate setup effort.
- **Workflows, automations, or features** activated beyond what setup creates automatically → genuine building behavior.
- **Usage volume above baseline** → real operational reliance.
- **Multiple team members** actively using the product.

Calibrate "deliberate vs incidental" by judgment based on what's known about the product and the org's use case. Org-specific definitions come from Setup.

Signal stack for post-conversion context (ranked):
1. **Product depth signals** above.
2. **Meeting completed** — extract: expansion topics, new departments mentioned, budget conversations.
3. **Post-signup website visits** — pricing pages signal upgrade intent; docs signal active usage.
4. **Conversational interactions** — advanced questions signal depth.
5. **Social engagement** — advocacy or deepening relationship signal.

---

### Step 2 — Assess expansion potential (Q2)

Research the account. Compare current product usage against total addressable usage within the account. The key question: how much headroom is left?

**Expansion headroom** = the gap between current product footprint and total addressable usage within the account — additional seats, teams, divisions, or use cases not yet covered.

Research explicitly:
- **Team size** relevant to the product's use case — how many people at this org could plausibly use the product?
- **GTM maturity signals** — funding rounds, relevant tools in tech stack, leadership present (seniority of active users and stakeholders), growth trajectory.
- **Teams or divisions** not yet using the product.
- **Growth signals** — recent hiring, funding, expansion into new markets.

Form a judgment on expansion potential; org-specific thresholds from Setup sharpen this.

---

### Step 3 — Apply the scoring frame

Use the parent's 2×2, adapted for post-conversion:

- Deep usage + Large expansion headroom → **Gold**
- Deep usage + Small expansion headroom → **Silver**
- Shallow usage + Large expansion headroom → **Silver**
- Shallow usage + Small expansion headroom → **Bronze**

Return:
