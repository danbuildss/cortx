## Instructions

Evaluate whether a closed-lost account showing new activity is worth re-engaging. A new signal alone is not sufficient — what matters is whether the underlying conditions that caused the loss have changed.

---

### Step 1 — Pull full relationship context

From account memory and CRM, retrieve:
- When the deal was lost and at what stage.
- The recorded reason for the loss.
- Which personas were engaged.
- Whether the prior champion is still at the company.

Also retrieve any **prior warm engagement** that didn't result in a deal — positive outreach replies, webinar/event registrations, conference attendance, booth visits. These signal relationship warmth that predates the loss and should inform the re-engagement framing.

An account that replied positively to outreach before going cold is in a different position than one that went completely silent. Surface the full warm history, not just the closed-lost record.

If no close-lost data is available: proceed with reduced confidence. Require **two** meaningful change signals instead of one before recommending re-engagement.

---

### Step 2 — Assess what has changed

The following constitute meaningful changes that justify re-engagement consideration:

- **New leadership** — prior champion left, new economic buyer arrived, or the blocker is gone.
- **Competitor displacement** — they dropped the vendor they chose over the org.
- **Funding event** — new budget or GTM investment.
- **New signal addressing the close-lost reason** — e.g. now hiring for a function that addresses the pain point that caused the loss.
- **Time elapsed >12 months** — sufficient for priorities to reset.
- **New inbound from a different person** — suggests broader interest than the prior champion.

---

### Step 3 — Score

**No meaningful change present** → return Bronze: `Closed-lost signal, no material change detected. Monitor only.` Skip Step 4.

**Meaningful change present** → apply the parent's Q1/Q2 frame with full awareness of the prior relationship.

- **Q1 (intent).** Weight the new signal against what was present at time of loss. A new first-party signal from a named individual is high intent. Third-party signals alone cap at Silver.
- **Q2 (deal size).** Research the account. Judge deal size using team size relevant to the product's use case, funding stage, company size, and growth trajectory. Reason from research until org-specific thresholds are baked in by Setup.

---

### Step 4 — Re-engagement framing

Only runs if tier is Silver or Gold.

Return alongside the standard fields:
- **What changed** — the specific hook that makes re-engagement timely now.
- **Prior relationship acknowledgment** — what to reference from the prior deal. Lead with honesty, not pitch. Do not pretend the prior conversation didn't happen.
- **Recommended first-touch angle** — one sentence: what to say and why it lands now given the change.

The framing is resurrection-first — relationship reopen, not new pitch.

---

### Return to parent

Return:
- Tier
- Mode (Re-engagement)
- Signal stack, ranked strongest first
- Persona quality
- Deal size assessment (one sentence)
- Reasoning applied
- Change delta (closed-lost context → current state)
- If Silver or Gold: re-engagement framing block (Step 4)
