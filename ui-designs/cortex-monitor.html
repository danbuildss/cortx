Alerts
Schema failure on any endpoint
Fires when a response fails schema validation. Immediate notification — schema failures indicate your handler is returning unexpected structure.
Channels: Slack #incidents · email
Payment latency above 3s
Fires when USDC settlement exceeds 3 seconds. Usually indicates on-chain congestion affecting your service's reliability for paying users.
Channels: Slack #infra
Endpoint unreachable
Fires on connection error or DNS failure. Immediate page if PagerDuty is connected.
Channels: PagerDuty · email
Delivery rate below 95% (24h)
Fires when 24h rolling paid delivery drops below 95% for any monitored service.
Channels: email
